Production building with warehouse, administration, showroom, undeveloped area and 177.5 kWp photovoltaic

We offer for sale an exceptional industrial property in the village of Ivachnová, Ružomberok district, with a direct connection to the D1 highway. The building has a built-up area of ​​2,132 m² and an additional undeveloped area of ​​1,695 m², which provides excellent opportunities for possible expansion or external handling and parking.

The key added value is its own photovoltaic power plant with an output of 177.5 kWp, which significantly reduces operating costs and increases the energy self-sufficiency of the operation. The building is also equipped with a natural gas connection, which opens up additional options for production, heating or technological processes.

The building has a flexible internal division, which allows for quick adjustment of the premises according to the current needs of the new owner — without the need for major construction investments.

Layout

The building is divided into the following functional zones:

• Production hall

• Material input

• Dispatch

• Production changing rooms, showers

• Storage areas of area, Cold storage and regular warehouses

• Administrative part (offices) of area

• Corridor

• Showroom

Advantages for the investor

• Flexible internal division — the premises can be quickly modified without major construction investments

• Low operating costs thanks to photovoltaics with an output of 177.5 kWp

• The building is ready for immediate operational use

• Direct connection to the D1 highway — excellent accessibility for truck and passenger transport

• Natural gas connection — ideal for heating, production or technological processes

• Undeveloped area of ​​1,695 m² — space for expansion, handling or parking

• Multifunctional complex — production, warehouse, admin and presentation areas

• Restaurant directly at the building — staff catering provided without the need to deal with corporate catering
